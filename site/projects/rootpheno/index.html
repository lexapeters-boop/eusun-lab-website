RootPheno

Building data-driven capacity for advanced root phenotyping

The limited ability to access root trait information due to the opaque soil medium and complex species composition remains a bottleneck in agroecological research.

Recent efforts to establish root infrastructure such as (i) multispectral imaging systems for species differentiation and (ii) microCT (computed tomography) for 3D root model construction, will benefit a wide range of researchers. However, these advanced systems require streamlined data processing and analytic pipelines. Without such data-driven solutions, the feasibility and usability of the root infrastructure will decline, and only a few research groups with specialists will be able to fully leverage its potential.

To address this, we propose developing advanced root phenotyping capacity by creating two data-driven pipelines that can be used by non-data specialists, such as crop and soil researchers.
